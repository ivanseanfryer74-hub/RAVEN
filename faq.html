Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within 3–7 business days depending on your location.

Can I return a product?

Yes. Products can be returned within the return period as long as they are unworn and in their original condition.

What payment methods do you accept?

MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and Credit/Debit Cards.

How can I contact RAVEN?

You can use the Contact page to send us a message and we'll respond as soon as possible.
